# Landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: founders, hiring managers and heads of talent at 20–500 person companies hiring for any AI-using profession (today: product manager, business analyst, sales, QA / SDET; more to come). Action: Start free (links to the app's /login; magic link, no password, no card). Free in early access. Proof: only what LANDING-BRIEF.md section 2 lists; no customers, metrics, pricing tiers, certifications. All product UI on the page is authored HTML, labelled as an example session, until real screenshots land.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page shows one mistake the AI made on purpose and whether the candidate caught it, in any profession. It does not use the usual layout of a headline, three feature cards and a logo strip, and it shows no claims the product cannot back.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite ground #111418 with 1px slate-700 hairlines, raised panels #1a1e24/#232830, one pencil-blue accent #3d68e8, teal #19a28b only for "Caught", amber #e0a04a only for "look here". Schibsted Grotesk set tight for display and UI, JetBrains Mono only inside code. Panels look like the replay app: title bar, timeline with key-moment ticks, file tree, editor, AI chat.

STORY: The take-home is broken by AI → Proofwork plants mistakes and records everything → visitor sees Caught vs Kept → believes the signal is verifiable → Start free.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left-aligned headline at about 4.5rem, one line of support, Start free button with the magic-link note beneath, and a row of profession pills. Below it, a full-width replay panel of a document-based product manager session, tilted back. Its chat answer is flagged "Planted error · Caught" above the 900px fold.

FORM: Category standard (Linear craft), pinned by the owner in PRODUCT.md; standing exit, no roll. Seed key: none (brief-pinned). Signature interaction: sticky scroll walk-through with a profession switcher. The whole session is ghosted in and resolves step by step to Caught or Missed.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
